How it works

Five days, five real conversations

No app to install, no vocabulary lists to memorize on your own. Here's exactly what the 5-day pilot looks like, day by day.

What the 5 days cover

A real walkthrough of the pilot's actual content — this is the same curriculum every participant gets.

1

Greetings

Say hello, introduce yourself, and thank someone — the three phrases you'll actually use in every conversation that follows.

2

Numbers & counting

Enough to order, ask a price, or tell someone your age without freezing up.

3

Family

Talk about who you live with and who's important to you — a natural next topic once you can say hello.

4

Food & drink

Order at a restaurant, say something's delicious, ask for water — the phrases from the sample above.

5

Week 1 review

Combine everything into one voice message — greet, order, and react — plus a short check-in on how the week felt.

Stick around after day 5 and the curriculum keeps going — directions, making plans, small talk, and more.

Frequently asked

Is the 5-day trial really free?

Yes. No card, no signup form — you just message on WhatsApp to start, and the first 5 days cost nothing.

I don't know any Korean at all. Is that okay?

That's who this is built for. Day 1 starts from zero — your first word is literally "hello."

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everything happens inside WhatsApp, which you almost certainly already have open every day.

Who gives the feedback — a person or an AI?

A real person listens to your voice messages and replies. See the About page for who that is.

What happens after day 5?

You can simply stop — no charge, no follow-up pressure. If you want to keep going, there's a paid monthly plan that continues the same daily format, cancel anytime.

What languages is feedback given in?

English, with Georgian support for the current Tbilisi pilot group. If you'd prefer another language, just ask — happy to figure something out.
