How it works

Four small steps, once a day.

1

Today's sentence arrives

A short WhatsApp message with 3–4 new Korean phrases and a sentence to practice.

2

Practice it out loud

Record yourself saying the sentence and send it back as a voice message.

3

Get pronunciation feedback

A short, honest note on what you got right and what to adjust — every single day.

4

Review yesterday's phrase

After you send your voice message, the reply brings back yesterday's key phrase so it sticks.
